About

A swing option lets the holder exercise a variable number of times over its life, each time locking in a fixed volume at a fixed strike, subject to a minimum and maximum total number of exercises. The holder's problem is to decide, at every date and every level of remaining capacity, whether the spot price is high enough to exercise now or better left for later.

The surface above is the solution to that problem: for each trading date and each cumulative volume already exercised, it shows the price S* above which immediate exercise is optimal. Where no finite threshold exists, exercise is forced by the contract's minimum-volume constraint and the surface shows a gap.

Change the contract and model parameters on the left to re-solve for a different commodity, strike, or capacity schedule.
